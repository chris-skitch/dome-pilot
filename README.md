# Dome Pilot

A remote control app for iPhone and iPad for the De Agostini / Fanhome
**Build Your Own R2-D2** 1:2 scale part-work model.

The model's original companion app is no longer available, which leaves the finished
model without its camera view, driving controls, sounds and routines. Dome Pilot is a
new, independent app that controls the model over Wi-Fi.

**Status:** being prepared for the App Store. This page will link to it when it is live.

This repository holds the app's documentation and its issue tracker. The source code is
not published here yet.

## What it does

- Drive with an on-screen joystick, or with any game controller paired with your device
- See through the model's camera, take photos and record video
- Turn the dome, play every built-in sound and run every built-in routine
- Build, save and share your own sequences of actions and sounds
- Works on your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) or connected straight to the model
- Step-by-step set-up with pictures of which button to press and which light to watch
- A Stop button that is always in the same place
- Siri and Shortcuts
- A built-in demo, so you can try it without the model
- No account, no ads, no tracking

## What you need

- The completed model, with its electronics fitted and working
- An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later
- For home Wi-Fi use, a 2.4 GHz network. The model cannot join 5 GHz networks.

## Problems and ideas

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/chris-skitch/dome-pilot/issues/new/choose).
For a connection problem, the app's diagnostics help a great deal: in the app go to
Settings, Help, Share diagnostics. Pairing ids and Wi-Fi passwords are masked in what it
shares.

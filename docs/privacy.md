# Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

**Dome Pilot collects no data.**

## What the app does with information

- **It talks only to your model.** The app connects to your model over your own local
  network, or over the model's own Wi-Fi network. It has no servers. Nothing is sent to
  the developer or to anyone else.
- **No accounts, advertising or analytics.** The app contains no tracking or analytics
  code and no third-party software kits.
- **Local network access.** The app asks for this permission so it can find your model
  on your Wi-Fi and connect to it. It is used for nothing else.
- **Photos and videos.** Pictures and recordings you take through the model's camera are
  saved to your own photo library, and only when you ask. The app can add to your
  library; it cannot read it.
- **Your Wi-Fi password.** When you put the model on your home Wi-Fi, the password you
  type is turned into a code on screen for the model's camera to read. It is not saved.
- **What is stored on your device.** Your settings, your saved models, your sequences,
  your controller button choices, and a random pairing id that the model uses to
  recognise your device. These stay on your device and in its own backups.
- **Connection log.** The app keeps a small log of connection events (connected, lost,
  reconnecting) on your device to help diagnose problems. It contains no commands and no
  camera pictures, and it stays on your device.
- **Diagnostics.** If you choose Share diagnostics, the app prepares a text report of
  the messages exchanged with your model, with pairing ids and Wi-Fi passwords masked,
  and hands it to the share sheet. It goes only where you send it.
- **Siri and Shortcuts.** If you use them with Dome Pilot, your requests are handled by
  Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. The app receives only the action to perform.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

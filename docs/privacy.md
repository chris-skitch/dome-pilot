# Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

**Dome Pilot collects no data.**

## What the app does with information

- **It talks only to your model, on your own network.** The app connects to your model
  over your local network, or over the model's own Wi-Fi network. It has no servers.
  Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and nothing leaves your network.
- **Finding your model.** To find the model, the app briefly tries each address on your
  own local network to see whether a model answers there. It sends nothing about you
  when it does.
- **No accounts, advertising or analytics.** The app contains no tracking or analytics
  code and no third-party software kits.
- **Local network access.** The app asks for this permission so it can find your model
  on your Wi-Fi and connect to it. It is used for nothing else.
- **Photos and videos.** Pictures and recordings you take through the model's camera are
  saved to your own photo library, and only when you ask. The app can add to your
  library; it cannot read it.
- **Your Wi-Fi password.** When you put the model on your home Wi-Fi, the password you
  type is turned into a code on screen for the model's camera to read. It is not saved.
- **What is stored on your device.** Your settings, your saved models, your sequences,
  your controller button choices, and a random pairing id that the model uses to
  recognise your device. These stay on your device and in its own backups. The pairing
  id is kept in the device's keychain, so it can remain after the app is deleted.
- **What your model is told.** When you pair, the app gives your model that pairing id
  and your device's name (for example "Sam's iPhone"). The model keeps them in its list
  of paired devices, which other devices paired with the same model can see.
- **Connection log.** The app keeps a small log on your device to help diagnose
  problems: when it connected and to which address on your network, when and why a
  connection was lost, when the model was stopped and why, and when a routine started.
  It contains no driving commands and no camera pictures. It stays on your device and in
  its backups; the app never sends it anywhere.
- **Diagnostics.** If you choose Share diagnostics, the app prepares a text report of
  the messages exchanged with your model, with pairing ids and Wi-Fi passwords masked,
  and hands it to the share sheet. It goes only where you send it. The report includes
  your model's name and local address, the name of your Wi-Fi network, and the names of
  other devices paired with the model, so read it before you share it.
- **Siri and Shortcuts.** If you use them with Dome Pilot, your requests are handled by
  Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. The app receives only the action to perform.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

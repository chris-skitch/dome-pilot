# <img src="images/icons/siri.svg" width="30" height="30" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> Siri and Shortcuts

Dome Pilot works with Siri and the Shortcuts app. You don't need to set anything up: once
the app is installed, its shortcuts are ready.

## What you can ask

| Shortcut | What it does | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Play a sound | Plays one of your model's sounds | "Play Yes! with Dome Pilot" or "Make a sound with Dome Pilot" |
| Run an action | An arm, a shake of the head, a light, a display or a projector | "Run Shake head with Dome Pilot" |
| Run a sequence | Runs one of your saved sequences | "Run a sequence with Dome Pilot" |
| <img src="images/icons/stop.svg" width="18" height="18" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> Stop | Stops your model straight away | "Stop my model with Dome Pilot" |
| <img src="images/icons/battery.svg" width="18" height="18" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> Check battery | Tells you how much battery is left | "How is my model's battery in Dome Pilot" |

If you don't name a sound, action or sequence, Siri asks which one you mean.

## Before you start

- <img src="images/icons/charging.svg" width="18" height="18" alt="" style="vertical-align: middle"> **Your model must be switched on and off its charger.** While it charges it leaves the
  Wi-Fi, so nothing can reach it.
- **The shortcut opens Dome Pilot.** It brings the app to the front, connects to your
  model (this takes a few seconds), then does what you asked. If it can't connect, Siri
  says "Open Dome Pilot and connect to your model first."
- If your model doesn't respond, Siri says so and you can try again.

## Nothing that drives your model

On purpose, Siri and Shortcuts can't make your model travel across the floor. Actions
that move it aren't offered, and sequences with a driving step are left out of the list.
Start those from inside the app, where you can see the model. **Stop always works.**

## Making your own shortcuts

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app and tap **+**.
2. Search for **Dome Pilot** and pick an action, for example **Play a sound**.
3. Choose the sound, action or sequence.
4. Give the shortcut a name. That name is what you say to Siri.

A few ideas:

- **Hello on arrival:** an automation that plays "Sing-song" when you get home. Your
  phone shows Dome Pilot when it runs.
- **Bedtime:** a shortcut that plays "Humming alone" and then runs Stop.
- **One button:** add a shortcut to your Home Screen or Action button to play your
  favourite sound.

Sequences are picked when you build the shortcut. Siri can't choose one by name from what
you say, so make one shortcut per sequence and name each one.

# Support and feedback

## Something isn't working

1. Try the [troubleshooting](troubleshooting) page first. Most problems are the model
   being on the wrong kind of Wi-Fi, or in the other Wi-Fi mode.
2. If that doesn't fix it, [open an issue](https://github.com/chris-skitch/dome-pilot/issues/new/choose)
   and choose **Problem report**.

A report is much easier to act on with:

- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead
- your device (for example iPhone 16 Pro Max) and its iOS version
- the Dome Pilot version, shown in Settings, About Dome Pilot
- whether the model was on your home Wi-Fi (back light green) or in direct mode (back
  light yellow)
- for connection problems, the app's diagnostics: Settings, Help, Share diagnostics.
  Pairing ids and Wi-Fi passwords are masked in what it shares. Please read it before
  attaching it: it includes your model's name and its address on your network, the name
  of your Wi-Fi network, and the names of other devices paired with the model. Issues
  here are public.

## Ideas

[Open an issue](https://github.com/chris-skitch/dome-pilot/issues/new/choose) and choose
**Idea or request**.

## What this app cannot fix

Dome Pilot is a replacement for the model's phone app. It cannot repair the model's own
electronics or change its built-in software, and it is not connected with the model's
publisher. For missing parts or faults with the kit itself, contact the company you
bought it from.
